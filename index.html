💬 Fresher FAQs



Are laptops mandatory for 1st year?
Not necessarily from day one, but a laptop can become useful for coding, assignments, projects and other academic work.
Is hostel accommodation guaranteed?
Hostel availability depends on the university's allotment process and available seats. Apply as early as possible and check the latest official instructions.
How are the hostel facilities?
Hostel experiences can vary. Students should be prepared with basic personal essentials and should verify the latest conditions directly with the university.
How are the classrooms and labs?
Facilities vary by department and laboratory. Check your department's current infrastructure and equipment during orientation.
What is the university's status?
Gurukul Kangri is a Deemed-to-be University. Always verify current regulatory information from official university sources.
Is there a dress code?
Students should follow the university's current academic and event-related dress guidelines.
Where can I find the syllabus?
Use the dedicated Syllabus tab above for the FET syllabus and the official GK(DU) syllabus repository.
