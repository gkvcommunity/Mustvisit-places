💬 Fresher FAQs

Are laptops mandatory for 1st-year?
While not strictly mandatory for day one, you'll need one for coding assignments and projects by the middle of the first semester. A decent mid-range laptop is enough!
Is a hostel room guaranteed for freshers?
Not automatically. Hostel seats are limited and allotted strictly on a first-come, first-served basis. Generally, 1st-year students and a few 2nd-year students get accommodation. Apply as early as possible!
Honest review: How are the hostel facilities?
To be completely transparent, it's a basic survival experience. Students occasionally face issues with water supply, sanitation, and average mess food. We highly recommend being self-reliant and keeping dry snacks handy!
How are the classrooms and practical labs?
The engineering building infrastructure is decent. Practical labs and workshops are average—they meet all your basic academic requirements and get the job done, but don't expect ultra-modern tech parks.
How are the professors and teaching staff?
Most faculty members are very knowledgeable and supportive! Each branch typically has a staff strength of 6–7 teachers (around 2-3 permanent faculty, and the rest are contractual). Don't hesitate to reach out to them for help.
