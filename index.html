🗺️ Haridwar Student Guide

A dedicated guide containing places that GKDU students should explore during their stay.

Explore Must-Visit Places ↗
🙏 Respect the place: When visiting temples, ghats and other religious or heritage locations, follow local rules and maintain cleanliness.
Are laptops mandatory for first year?
Not necessarily from day one, but a laptop can be useful for programming assignments, projects and academic work.
Is hostel accommodation guaranteed?
Hostel availability depends on the university's allocation and available capacity. Check the latest official instructions before relying on hostel accommodation.
How are the hostel facilities?
Facilities are basic, so students should be prepared to manage their everyday essentials independently.
How are the classrooms and labs?
The engineering facilities provide the basic infrastructure needed for academic classes, practicals and workshops.
How are the professors?
Faculty experience can vary by department and subject. Students should make use of lectures, office hours and academic resources available through their department.
Is there a dress code?
Gurukul Kangri has a traditional academic environment. Students should follow the latest university guidelines regarding dress and conduct.
Where can I find the syllabus?
Use the Syllabus section above for FET syllabus and the university's syllabus archive.
