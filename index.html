Questions?

Things every fresher wants to know.

Are laptops mandatory for first year?
A laptop may not be necessary from the first day, but it can become useful for coding, assignments, projects and other academic work.
Is hostel accommodation guaranteed?
Hostel availability depends on available seats and the university's current allotment process. Check the latest official instructions before making arrangements.
Where can I find the FET syllabus?
Open the Syllabus section in the Resource Hub above. The FET GKV syllabus link is provided there.
Where can I find the syllabus for other courses?
The official GK(DU) old syllabus repository is available in the Syllabus section.
Where can I find previous year papers?
Use the Academic section to access the paper collection and FET previous papers.
Where can I find places to visit?
Open the Must Visit Places feature above for a dedicated student-oriented places guide.
Is this website official?
No. This is an unofficial student resource. Important university, examination, fee and administrative information should always be verified through official university sources.
